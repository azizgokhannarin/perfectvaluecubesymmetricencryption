# Research Goal

The project goal is to expose the frozen Perfect Value Cube keyed construction line as a coherent, reproducible symmetric-encryption research candidate without hiding its assumptions or importing an established cryptographic primitive into the design.

Success at this stage means:

- a single unambiguous public profile;
- exact reproducibility across implementations and platforms;
- clean provenance to frozen C1/M1/A1 components;
- public attack surfaces and non-claims;
- external cryptanalysis that can falsify or refine confidence in the construction.

It does not mean production deployment or a claim that the design has reached an established security level.
